The new store of Hao Xiang La supermarket in Shunyi District, Beijing has opened.

On the morning of September 16, 2026, the new store of Hao Xiang La supermarket in Shunyi District, Beijing officially opened. This store is located in a densely populated area, right between two residential areas.


This store has for the first time used a building that was originally a villa but was rented out because no one bought it, and which is located opposite other stores, as its store premises.
